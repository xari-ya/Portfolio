JellyCast shoreline data

The Sri Lanka shoreline derives from GSHHG (formerly GSHHS), intermediate-resolution data distributed with Basemap. Authors: Paul Wessel and Walter H. F. Smith. Upstream project.

Portfolio adaptations select the main Sri Lanka and offshore rings, round coordinates, triangulate and project the outline for WebGL, SVG and CSS. The derived geographic coordinates remain under the GNU Lesser General Public License. Editable coordinates and their notice are distributed in the shoreline data companion; these duplicate the existing rendering coordinates without changing them.
