# Car collection and office details

The supplied OBJ and Alpine FBX archive are ingested offline by tools/optimize-office-cars.py. Install fast-simplification in the shared Python environment; NumPy supplies array operations. The original source files remain on disk and are ignored by Git. No OBJ, FBX, source textures or ZIP is requested by the website.

The deployed cars total 368,058 bytes and 40,533 triangles, compared with approximately 266 MB of supplied sources. Source material boundaries, curved bodywork and wheel silhouettes are retained; subpixel details, hidden Alpine cabin/engine/spare-wheel geometry and a detached projector prop are omitted. Material seams are welded before QEM reduction, body borders are protected, winding is repaired, and body/glass face orientation is checked before 16-bit position quantization. The missing OBJ companion MTL means paint/glass/trim finishes are reconstructed; original texture fidelity is not claimed.

communications-room/cars.js loads the two .car files once on office construction, reconstructs indexed meshes and uploads through the existing scene API. The shelf anchors remain the existing groups at 0.52 m model length. Native pointer picking covers the complete car display, and the existing camera controller blends into one view containing both cars and the monitor. Dragging, scrolling or navigation restores normal framing; no exit button is added. A keyboard-accessible inspection action uses the existing focused-action styling. Offscreen wall contact anchors are hidden during desktop inspection, with mobile contacts retained in the reading panel. The foreground left wall is extended only to provide a completed background for the inspection frame.

The PC beneath the cars shows a captured image of the actual homepage. The office laptop uses an Ubuntu desktop painted to one static texture, passed through the renderer's existing workspace override; its geometry and the hall's original screen remain intact. The laptop is placed at [1.65, 0.868, -1.29], yaw PI/2 - 0.22; the nearby book stack moves toward the center to avoid overlap.

A five-ball Newton's cradle replaces the desk's rectangular lamp prop. Its constrained pendula use fixed 1/240 s substeps and elastic adjacent contacts in the existing room update loop. Only five pivot transforms move. Clicking the cradle releases the end ball. Reduced motion settles the balls and re-enabling motion starts a clean release. A five-minute numerical simulation verifies bounded motion and transfer to the opposite end.

The parallel lounge moves 0.55 m toward the camera. The front-right plant is removed. A shallow horizontal planter sits below the Get in Touch panel. The three left planting areas use a restrained material lift for visibility. The central floor monogram is replaced by a woven carpet with a bound edge.

The supplied network-connections image is copied unchanged to assets/network-connections.png. Only blank image margins are cropped during board composition. The diagram spans 30% of the board width and 56% of its height, extending above the divider in the full right-hand column; the divider stops at the text-column edge. The display's graphic and contact-row spacing leave clearance around the laptop. THE LIVING WORKSHOP is removed from the board and upper-left office heading, which now reads MY OFFICE & COMMUNICATIONS.
